02 / Engineering Approach

Trust starts with
the boundaries.

Clear responsibilities, traceable evidence, and honest limitations shape how I approach a system.

01 / SCOPE

Define permission before capability.

REVENANT’s conceptual workflow begins with authorization and scope, before assessment. The intended boundary is part of the architecture.

02 / EVIDENCE

Keep evidence connected to context.

SPECTRE’s design centers on case-linked evidence and provenance: where information came from matters alongside what it shows.

03 / PURPOSE

Design for the actual environment.

ShadowWatch is a training simulator. Keeping practice distinct from live operations makes the purpose and limits of the tool clear.

04 / VERIFICATION

Make claims that evidence can support.

I separate documented scope from planned work and make limitations explicit. Verification should explain what a system does, and where the evidence ends.

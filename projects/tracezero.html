Overview

TRACEZERO centers on evidence integrity, scoped authorization, and chain of custody. Development is documented through Phase 06, before the unified timeline. The project is still in progress.

Project scope

  • SHA-256 verification for evidence integrity.
  • Scoped access to investigation resources.
  • Chain-of-custody records and evidence handling.
  • Concurrency and archive-safety considerations.
